Welcome to Panchwati

Where every function feels truly royal.

From the lit floral gate to the chandelier corridor, the rose-wall stage and the open lawn under the stars, Panchwati is built for Indian celebrations. Your baraat, jaimala, dinner and dance all happen in one place, and your guests never have to move venues.
